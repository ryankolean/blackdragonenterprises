About

Angus Peacock

Royal Navy veteran. Rugby coach and coach educator on four continents. Life coach, mentor and author.

Angus works in English, French and Mandarin Chinese, and has spent two decades helping people and teams find a level they did not know they had. His coaching philosophy starts from sisu: the Finnish idea of resolve under pressure, and an investment in your own longevity.
