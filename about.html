About

Angus Peacock

Coach, mentor and author. Twenty years of leading and developing people across business, sport and the military, on four continents.

Background

Where the experience comes from

Angus served in the Royal Navy before building a career in coaching. He has coached rugby for more than two decades across North America, South America, Europe and Asia, and holds World Rugby Level 3 and USA Rugby Level 400 coaching credentials as well as accreditation as a coach educator.

Alongside sport he works as a life coach and mentor, and in business on leadership, performance and project delivery. He speaks English, French and Mandarin Chinese.

His book, SISU: A Series of Epic Adventures (Tactical 16), tells the true story of turning personal trauma into a journey across Europe and Asia. The same resolve sits at the centre of his coaching.
